Foreign Withholding Tax (FWT) Intelligence Engine

Cross-border tax rules, Level 1 & Level 2 mechanics, Canada–US treaty exemptions, and account drag calculations
1. Level 1: Direct Foreign Tax
Withholding applied directly by the foreign source country (e.g. US IRS withholds 15% on US dividends; European and Asian nations withhold ~8–12%).
2. Level 2: Fund Wrapper Tax
A second 15% US withholding tax triggered when a Canadian ETF holds a US-listed ETF wrapper (e.g. XAW holding ITOT/IEFA) before distributing to Canadians.
3. Canada–US Tax Treaty Exemption (RRSP Only)
US-listed ETFs holding US stocks directly (e.g. VTI) held in an RRSP/RRIF are 100% exempt from US withholding. Canadian-listed ETFs (XUU, XEQT, VEQT) cannot pass this through to RRSPs.
4. Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) in Taxable Accounts
In non-registered accounts, foreign tax reported on T3/T5 slips is recoverable as a tax credit on line 40500 of your T1. In TFSA and RESP accounts, foreign withholding is permanently unrecoverable.
